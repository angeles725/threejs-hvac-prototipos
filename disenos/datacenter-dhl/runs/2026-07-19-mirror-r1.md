UI, B43 light palette. Every value stays DERIVED from the deterministic sims; simulated feeds say

## Honest cuts / deviations (logged)

- **Resolved episodes = honest 0.** DHL's non-warn units sit safely below their thresholds by
  margin construction and the one warn unit stays warn all day, so the real day-walk finds no band
  re-entries. The machinery is real and tested; the count is a truthful zero (like the sibling's
  Sistema=0), surfaced in the Resueltas (hoy) card and the Derivación aside copy.
- **Sensación térmica has no intra-day trend.** DHL's exterior ambient is a single fixed source
  (the one the dry cooler rejects against), so feels-like is a single derived number. The sibling's
  8-sample half-hour trend sparkline is cut; the card documents it ("sin variación intradía").
- **Energía per-phase columns cut.** The simulated meter exposes demand (kW) per instrumented unit
  only — no tension-per-phase or frequency — so the sibling's V / FP columns do not exist here
  (documented in the table note).
- **No LoRaWAN / UC100 / RS-485 topology.** DHL has no field-bus fabric, so the cinemex bus donut
  and RS-485 rack aside are re-authored to DHL truths: Ventiladores' donut is "Fuentes por arreglo"
  (CRAC/In-row/Dry), Cuarto's aside is the chilled-water loop summary.
- **HVAC has no per-unit setpoint.** The sibling's ±1.5 °C "fuera de banda vs consigna" becomes the
  truthful "unidades fuera de banda" = units past their status threshold; "Modo" filter becomes the
  truthful status filter.
- **Comparisons are seeded plausibilities** anchored to the one modeled day/month (same documented
  convention as the sibling); every rendered percentage audits against its own kWh pair.
