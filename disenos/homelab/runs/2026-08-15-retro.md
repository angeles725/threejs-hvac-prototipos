# homelab — design3d retro (2026-08-15, in-run observation)

**Scope:** ongoing homelab heavy run (rack-cabinet asset, P4 blockout). Captured mid-run at the
user's explicit request — NOT the P8 close. Propose-only (Hard Rule 6): nothing here is applied to
the kit; the user promotes.

## Observation (evidence-pinned) — the gated loop reproduces "by-eye" quality, objectively and on disk

An older, well-regarded design — `disenos/catalog/datacenter/contencion-pasillo/` — achieves rack
doors whose perforated mesh READS correctly. But it is a MONOLITH on a CDN with a `design-spec.yaml`
and NO `runs/progress.yaml`: no recorded gate ladder. Its quality was reached and judged BY EYE.

The homelab rack blockout hit the SAME feature adversarially and objectively:
- `rack-cabinet/runs/blockout-attempt1.review.json`: `perforated-mesh-front-door` scored **0.10**
  (rendered as an opaque flat panel) → blockout **FAIL 0.46**.
- Correction (attempt 2): a real alphaMap perforation (~50% open, `alphaTest 0.5`, `DoubleSide`) →
  the mesh reads AND the rails behind show through.

So the blind-review loop CAUGHT the exact quality gap that the ungated design happened to get right
by eye, and left the finding + the fix as auditable artifacts on disk (`review.json`,
`progress.yaml`). The value is not only the outcome — it is the RECORD of why it passed.

## Proposed (propose-only — user promotes)
- **LEARNING (stage):** "A gated blind-review loop reproduces the quality an experienced author
  reaches by eye, but as an ON-DISK, auditable verdict; the recorded artifact — not just the render —
  is the deliverable that ungated monoliths lack." → candidate for `LEARNINGS.md` §Staged.
- **No kit DELTA:** this VALIDATES the existing gate; it does not change it.

## Honesty
A positive validation, pinned to two review JSONs and one capture set; a fresh reviewer can verify
against those artifacts. It does not invent a defect to look productive.
