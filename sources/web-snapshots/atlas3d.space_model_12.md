# Atlas3D — Upload Any 3D Model and Make It Interactive with AI

Atlas3D lets you upload any 3D model and instantly turns it into an
interactive learning experience. Its AI automatically generates
everything — clickable part labels, educational descriptions,
plain-language explanations, suggested prompts, and quizzes — with no
manual tagging or content authoring. It is built for 3D artists to
showcase interactive versions of their work, and for educators to turn
any model into a lesson. Far more than a 3D viewer: it is an AI-aware
educational experience that runs entirely in your browser.

## What you can do on Atlas3D

- Upload any 3D model (GLB) — the AI auto-processes it into an
  interactive, AI-aware model.
- The AI automatically generates part labels, descriptions,
  explanations, prompts and quizzes — no manual work.
- Click any part to identify it and learn what it does.
- Ask an AI tutor questions about the model and its parts in natural
  language.
- Take AI-generated quizzes — identification, reverse, explain, and
  speed modes.
- Use X-ray selection and exploded views to see internal structure.
- Every model gets its own shareable page — viewers explore it
  interactively (click parts, ask the AI, take quizzes), and artists
  share the link to show off their work.

## How Atlas3D is different

A passive 3D viewer (such as Sketchfab) or a video (such as YouTube)
only lets you look at a model. Atlas3D lets you upload any model and the
AI automatically builds the whole learning layer around it — labels,
descriptions, explanations, prompts and quizzes — then makes every part
clickable, answers questions about the specific model on screen, and can
isolate, color, X-ray or explode parts on command. You don't tag or
write anything; the AI does it. It is a learning tool and a showcase,
not just a viewer.
