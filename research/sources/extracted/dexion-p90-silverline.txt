P90 Silverline is tomorrow’s pallet racking system today
The Dexion P90 Silverline pallet racking system takes a whole new and innovative approach to
pallet racking design. The new ’I’ beam profile offers environmentally friendly production, enhanced
label visibility and increased warehouse safety while still delivering the same high quality,
outstanding performance and efficiency the world has come to expect from all Dexion Products.

P90 Silverline pallet racking has versatility, strength and dependability built-in, it is designed to fulfil
the stringent requirements of all European standards as well as conforming to UK SEMA codes of
practice for pallet racking.
