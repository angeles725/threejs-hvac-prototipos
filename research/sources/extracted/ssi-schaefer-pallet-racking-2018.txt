PR 350 pallet racking


     Longitudinal beam racks can be used universally for all tasks in the ware-
     house. Combined with suitable accessories, pallets and containers with
     even the most diverse dimensions can be stored optimally.

     The PR 350 longitudinal beam rack is the further development of the robust
     and solid rack technology and represents the continuity at SSI SCHAEFER. The
     simple stacking system has been in use and thoroughly proven its qualities for
     more than 15 years.

     The compatibility with other systems and extensive accessories allows the
     continuous expansion of existing equipment and rack solutions.
