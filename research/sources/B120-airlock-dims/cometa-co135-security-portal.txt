Mantrap with interlocking doors, designed as access control gate for: banks, financial institutes, companies, embassies, datacenters, prisons, miisteries, jewelleries and value protection areas. The second door opens only if control systems give positive result. Fully self-managed. Person transit automatically managed, with voice commands during the transit steps (multilanguage messages). It can be equipped with metal detector and access control systems such as: badge readers, RFIDs, NFCs, fingerprints and/or anti-masking systems.
