Description The Circlelock Solo is a high-security portal specifically designed for controlled single-person access. It is ideal for sensitive areas where security cannot be compromised. This state-of-the-art security solution integrates seamlessly into any building environment, offering a sophisticated design without sacrificing functionality. Features and benefits: High-security portal for internal or external use. Supports bi-directional traffic and multi-factor authentication. Prevents tailgating. Optional one or two-way piggybacking detection with StereoVision. Seamless integration with third-party access control systems. Available with RC3-rated Burglar Resistance package or Bullet Resistance package (BR4NS). Fail safe operation as standard, fail secure is optional. Wide range of materials and finishes available.
