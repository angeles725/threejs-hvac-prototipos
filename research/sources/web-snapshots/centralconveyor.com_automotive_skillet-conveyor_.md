<div class="fl-module-content fl-node-content">

<div class="fl-rich-text">

The Skillet system is normally used in General Assembly operations for
the trim and final line operations and is derived from two systems, the
Skid system and the Pallet system.

The Skillet conveyor systems utilize platforms (skillets) to transport
materials or products through various assembly operations. The skillets
provide access around the perimeter of the product, enabling the worker
to travel with the product as it is conveyed through the various stages
of assembly.

The product is carried either on fixtures set to a fixed height or a
lift table that may be either mechanically or electrically driven. The
adjustable height skillet allows the skillet to be raised or lowered for
production operations and transfers.
