<div id="mwAg"
class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux"
style="display:none" about="#mwt1" typeof="mw:Transclusion"
mw="{&quot;parts&quot;:[{&quot;template&quot;:{&quot;target&quot;:{&quot;wt&quot;:&quot;short description&quot;,&quot;href&quot;:&quot;./Template:Short_description&quot;},&quot;params&quot;:{&quot;1&quot;:{&quot;wt&quot;:&quot;Surface representing points of constant value within a volume&quot;}},&quot;i&quot;:0}}]}">

Surface representing points of constant value within a volume

</div>

<span class="mw-empty-elt" about="#mwt1"></span>

An **isosurface** is a three-dimensional analog of an
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contour_line" id="mwBQ"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Contour line">isoline</a>. It is a
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_(mathematics)" id="mwBg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Surface (mathematics)">surface</a> that
represents points of a constant value (e.g.
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure" id="mwBw" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Pressure">pressure</a>,
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature" id="mwCA"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Temperature">temperature</a>,
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velocity" id="mwCQ" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Velocity">velocity</a>,
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density" id="mwCg" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Density">density</a>) within a
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume" id="mwCw" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Volume">volume</a> of space; in other words, it is a
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_set" id="mwDA" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Level set">level set</a> of a continuous
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Function_(mathematics)" id="mwDQ"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Function (mathematics)">function</a> whose
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_of_a_function" id="mwDg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Domain of a function">domain</a> is
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-space" id="mwDw" class="mw-redirect"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="3-space">3-space</a>.

The term *isoline* is also sometimes used for domains of more than 3
dimensions.<sup><a href="#cite_note-1" id="mwEg"><span id="mwEw"
class="mw-reflink-text"><span id="mwFA"
class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span id="mwFQ"

<div id="mwHA" class="section" mw-section-id="1"
aria-labelledby="Applications">

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2">

## Applications

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Applications")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

Isosurfaces are normally displayed using
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics" id="mwHg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Computer graphics">computer graphics</a>, and
are used as data visualization methods in
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_fluid_dynamics" id="mwHw"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Computational fluid dynamics">computational
fluid dynamics</a> (CFD), allowing engineers to study features of a
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_flow" id="mwIA"
class="mw-redirect" rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Fluid flow">fluid flow</a>
(gas or liquid) around objects, such as aircraft
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wing" id="mwIQ" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Wing">wings</a>. An isosurface may represent an individual
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_wave" id="mwIg" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Shock wave">shock wave</a> in
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersonic" id="mwIw"
class="mw-redirect" rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Supersonic">supersonic</a>
flight, or several isosurfaces may be generated showing a sequence of
pressure values in the air flowing around a wing. Isosurfaces tend to be
a popular form of visualization for volume datasets since they can be
rendered by a simple polygonal model, which can be drawn on the screen
very quickly.

In <a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging" id="mwJQ"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Medical imaging">medical imaging</a>,
isosurfaces may be used to represent regions of a particular
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density" id="mwJg" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Density">density</a> in a three-dimensional
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography" id="mwJw"
class="mw-redirect" rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Computed tomography">CT</a>
scan, allowing the visualization of internal
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organ_(anatomy)" id="mwKA"
class="mw-redirect" rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Organ (anatomy)">organs</a>,
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bone" id="mwKQ" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Bone">bones</a>, or other structures.

Numerous other disciplines that are interested in three-dimensional data
often use isosurfaces to obtain information about
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacology" id="mwKw"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Pharmacology">pharmacology</a>,
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry" id="mwLA" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Chemistry">chemistry</a>,
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics" id="mwLQ" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Geophysics">geophysics</a> and
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorology" id="mwLg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Meteorology">meteorology</a>.

</div>

## Implementation algorithms

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Implementation algorithms")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

<div id="mwMA" class="section" mw-section-id="3"
aria-labelledby="Marching_cubes">

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3">

### Marching cubes

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Marching cubes")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

The <a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marching_cubes" id="mwMg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Marching cubes">marching cubes</a> algorithm
was first published in the 1987 SIGGRAPH proceedings by Lorensen and
Cline,<sup><a href="#cite_note-2" id="mwMw"><span id="mwNA"
class="mw-reflink-text"><span id="mwNQ"
class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span id="mwNg"
class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></a></sup> and it creates a surface
by intersecting the edges of a
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_(computing)" id="mwNw"
class="mw-redirect" rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Data (computing)">data</a>
volume grid with the volume contour. Where the surface intersects the
edge the algorithm creates a vertex. By using a table of different
triangles depending on different patterns of edge intersections the
algorithm can create a surface. This algorithm has solutions for
implementation both on the CPU and on the GPU.

</div>

<div id="mwOA" class="section" mw-section-id="4"
aria-labelledby="Asymptotic_decider">

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3">

### Asymptotic decider

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Asymptotic decider")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

The <a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_decider" id="mwOg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Asymptotic decider">asymptotic decider</a>
algorithm was developed as an extension to
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marching_cubes" id="mwOw"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Marching cubes">marching cubes</a> in order to
resolve the possibility of ambiguity in it.

</div>

<div id="mwPA" class="section" mw-section-id="5"
aria-labelledby="Marching_tetrahedra">

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3">

### Marching tetrahedra

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Marching tetrahedra")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

The <a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marching_tetrahedra" id="mwPg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Marching tetrahedra">marching tetrahedra</a>
algorithm was developed as an extension to
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marching_cubes" id="mwPw"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Marching cubes">marching cubes</a> in order to
solve an ambiguity in that algorithm and to create higher quality output
surface.

</div>

<div id="mwQA" class="section" mw-section-id="6"
aria-labelledby="Surface_nets">

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3">

### Surface nets

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Surface nets")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

The Surface Nets algorithm places an intersecting vertex in the middle
of a volume voxel instead of at the edges, leading to a smoother output
surface.

</div>

<div id="mwQg" class="section" mw-section-id="7"
aria-labelledby="Dual_contouring">

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3">

### Dual contouring

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Dual contouring")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

The <a
href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_contouring?action=edit&amp;redlink=1"
id="mwRA" class="new" rel="mw:WikiLink" typeof="mw:LocalizedAttrs"
data-mw-i18n="{&quot;title&quot;:{&quot;lang&quot;:&quot;x-page&quot;,&quot;key&quot;:&quot;red-link-title&quot;,&quot;params&quot;:[&quot;Dual contouring&quot;]}}"
title="Dual contouring (page does not exist)">dual contouring</a>
algorithm was first published in the 2002 SIGGRAPH proceedings by Ju and
Losasso,<sup><a href="#cite_note-3" id="mwRQ"><span id="mwRg"
class="mw-reflink-text"><span id="mwRw"
class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span id="mwSA"
class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></a></sup> developed as an extension
to both
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_nets?action=edit&amp;redlink=1"
id="mwSQ" class="new" rel="mw:WikiLink" typeof="mw:LocalizedAttrs"
data-mw-i18n="{&quot;title&quot;:{&quot;lang&quot;:&quot;x-page&quot;,&quot;key&quot;:&quot;red-link-title&quot;,&quot;params&quot;:[&quot;Surface nets&quot;]}}"
title="Surface nets (page does not exist)">surface nets</a> and marching
cubes. It retains a
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polyhedron" id="mwSg"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Dual polyhedron">dual</a> vertex within the
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voxel" id="mwSw" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Voxel">voxel</a> but no longer at the center. Dual contouring
leverages the position and
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_(geometry)" id="mwTA"
rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Normal (geometry)">normal</a> of where the
surface crosses the edges of a voxel to interpolate the position of the
dual vertex within the voxel. This has the benefit of retaining sharp or
smooth surfaces where surface nets often look blocky or incorrectly
beveled.<sup><a href="#cite_note-4" id="mwTQ"><span id="mwTg"
class="mw-reflink-text"><span id="mwTw"
class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span id="mwUA"
class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></a></sup> Dual contouring often
uses surface generation that leverages
<a href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octree" id="mwUQ" rel="mw:WikiLink"
title="Octree">octrees</a> as an optimization to adapt the number of
triangles in output to the complexity of the surface.

</div>

aria-labelledby="Manifold_dual_contouring">

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3">

### Manifold dual contouring

<span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\[</span>[edit](/w/index.php?title=Isosurface&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Manifold dual contouring")<span class="mw-editsection-bracket">\]</span></span>

</div>

Manifold <a
href="//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_contouring?action=edit&amp;redlink=1"
id="mwVA" class="new" rel="mw:WikiLink" typeof="mw:LocalizedAttrs"
data-mw-i18n="{&quot;title&quot;:{&quot;lang&quot;:&quot;x-page&quot;,&quot;key&quot;:&quot;red-link-title&quot;,&quot;params&quot;:[&quot;Dual contouring&quot;]}}"
title="Dual contouring (page does not exist)">dual contouring</a>
includes an analysis of the octree neighborhood to maintain continuity
of the manifold surface
<sup><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwVQ"><span id="mwVg"
class="mw-reflink-text"><span id="mwVw"
class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span id="mwWA"
class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></a><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwWQ"><span id="mwWg"
class="mw-reflink-text"><span id="mwWw"
class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span id="mwXA"
class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></a><a href="#cite_note-7" id="mwXQ"><span id="mwXg"
class="mw-reflink-text"><span id="mwXw"
class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span id="mwYA"
class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></a></sup>

</div>

</div>
