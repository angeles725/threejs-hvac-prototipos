# Ribbon blender — preserved source extracts (proportions, not absolute sizes)

Fetched: 2026-08-08
Sources: mixers.com "Scale-up of Ribbon Blenders", destomy.com ribbon-blender design guide,
chemicalprocessing.com "Mixing How To: Ribbon Blenders", iqsdirectory ribbon-mixer article.
Marker: [CERT-web].

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| ribbon pitch : agitator diameter | 1 : 1 ("a 1:1 pitch to diameter is common") |
| outer ribbon width | ~10 % of diameter ("200 mm ... 10% of the diameter") |
| inner ribbon width | 200-300 mm on that same size, i.e. wider fraction than the outer |
| inner ribbon radial position | "about halfway between the outer ribbon and the center shaft" |
| ribbon-to-trough clearance | 3-5 mm for standard powders |
| trough shape | U-shaped, horizontal agitator |

## The defining fact — counter-pitched ribbons

> "The Outer Ribbon is pitched to move the material from the ends of the trough toward the center
> discharge valve, while the Inner Ribbon is pitched in the opposite direction, moving the material
> from the center back toward the ends."

This is the whole machine. Two nested helices turning on ONE shaft, pitched AGAINST each other, is
what produces the counter-current axial flow that mixes; it is also the detail a modeller gets
wrong, because building both helices with the same hand looks perfectly plausible and would simply
auger everything to one end. Note it also means the OUTER ribbon must reverse hand at mid-length
(both halves drive toward the centre), and the inner must reverse the other way.

## Not evidenced

Absolute size, trough length, shaft diameter, drive, discharge valve type and the bearing/seal
arrangement. All [INFER] in the design spec, chosen as a ~1 m3 unit.
