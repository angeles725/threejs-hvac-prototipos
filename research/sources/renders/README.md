# renders/

Screenshots and rendered stills of the prototypes produced during research runs
(design iterations, before/after QA captures, palette checks).

These are **artefacts of our own prototypes**, not snapshots of external web
sources, so they do NOT belong in `web-snapshots/` and are NOT registered in
`SOURCES.md` (which tracks cited external evidence with provenance hashes). They
were moved here on 2026-08-07 to unblock `verify-sources` (which flagged them as
orphans in `web-snapshots/`). No block cites any of them; they are kept for
historical reference only.
